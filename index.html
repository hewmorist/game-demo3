1 · Find the lock settings
          
Reset the dial. Turn anticlockwise and watch each wheel in the Lock Model. When its gate notch is near the top and the model says Setting readable or Gate centred, record its rounded whole-number wheel reading here: that is its lock setting. Half a division of positioning tolerance is allowed. Wheel readings start at zero on Reset and differ from the main dial reading. Record wheels 4 / 3 / 2 / 1.

          

            
            
            
            
          

          

        

        
2 · Discover gate positions and appearance numbers
          
Start anticlockwise from Reset at zero. Discover the gate positions for wheels 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 in alternating moves. Only the wheel you are observing needs to be aligned; other wheels may move and their recorded readings are kept. Half a division of positioning tolerance is allowed. Count each arrival at a gate position as an appearance. The first arrival requires movement, even if that number is zero. Record gate positions with two decimal places.

          
Starting direction: anticlockwise. Record each decimal gate position and its appearance number. These are discovery observations; the completed Dial Instructions use integer dial positions beyond each gate.
This lock needs an initial anticlockwise clearing move: turn at least once, stop with wheel 4’s gate centred, then reverse clockwise to set wheel 1. Record the clearing move in the first row.

          
After each observation, reverse for the next wheel. Record the observed gate position to two decimal places. Corrective turns are practice and do not change the established appearance number. Once all gate positions are recorded, the uninterrupted opening is checked automatically. The resulting Dial Instructions convert each gate position into an integer position beyond the gate with its own appearance number.
